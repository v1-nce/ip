## Sorting tasks

Sorts the task list by date, earliest first. Deadlines are ordered by
their due date and events by their start date; todos have no date, so
they are listed after all dated tasks. The new order is saved.

Example: `sort`

```
 Here are the tasks in your list, sorted:
 1.[E][ ] camp (from: Oct 10 2019 to: Oct 12 2019)
 2.[D][ ] pay bill (by: Oct 15 2019)
 3.[T][ ] read book
```
